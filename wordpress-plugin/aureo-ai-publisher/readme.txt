Enterprise automated daily AI business publisher powered by Google Gemini 2.5 & 2.0.

== Description ==
Aureo AI Daily Post Publisher creates 1-2 SEO-optimized business monographs, market analyses, civil engineering overviews, or architectural pieces every day automatically via WP-Cron.

= Features =
* Complete Google Gemini Model Suite (2.5 Flash, 2.5 Pro, 2.0 Flash, 1.5 Flash, 1.5 Pro).
* Live "Fetch My Models" Account Discovery.
* Granular Tone (Authoritative, Technical, Conversational, Luxury) & Length controls.
* Fully automated daily or twice-daily publishing via WP-Cron.
* Any custom business niche (e.g. construction, civil engineering, interior, SaaS, wealth).
* Automated featured image attachment from curated high-resolution photography.
* Instant "Generate Now" trigger from WordPress Admin.
* Autonomous Dynamic Synthesizer fallback for 100% publishing uptime.

== Installation ==
1. Upload aureo-ai-publisher.zip via Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin.
2. Activate the plugin.
3. Configure your API key & niche in **AI Auto Publisher** in the WordPress admin menu.
